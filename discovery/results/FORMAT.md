# Format of the results

Generated by `tools/s08_build.py`; do not edit by hand (change `decisions/` or the tools and rebuild).

- `INDEX.md` — one row per leitmotif (`LMnnn`), numbered by pattern length. Columns: statistical strength, notes in
  the pattern, occurrences, scenes, operas it appears in (A Rheingold, B Walkure, C Siegfried, D Gotterdammerung),
  where it first appears, and the reviewers' hints about the dramatic context (hints, not names).
- `leitmotifs/LMnnn.md` — readable page: the pattern (notes, durations in quarter notes, intervals in semitones) and a
  table of every occurrence. `leitmotifs/LMnnn.json` — the same as data:
  - `pattern_notes`, `pattern_durations`, `pattern_intervals`: one representative occurrence. The pattern is defined by
    intervals and the long/short profile, so occurrences are transposed and may vary slightly (exact matches agree on
    every interval and rhythm class; approximate matches allow up to 15 % of intervals and 25 % of rhythm classes to differ).
  - `member_candidates`: ids of the mined patterns merged into this leitmotif (they are listed in `work/` only; each is
    a variant, a fragment, or a pattern that always occurs together with the main one).
  - `occurrences[]`: `act` (e.g. `WWV086B-2`), `act_short`, `scene` (the annotated scene number within the act),
    `pos0`/`pos1` (score position `measure.fraction`, measure numbering as in the dataset annotations, fraction = part of
    the measure), `time` (m:ss in the Karajan1966 recording, linear interpolation of the manual measure times), `streams`
    (where the pattern was found: T1/T2 top note of right/left hand, B1/B2 bottom note, L111/L112/L211/L212 single
    voices), `match` (exact or approximate with a distance), `notes` and `durations` as played at that occurrence (first
    48 notes), `text` (what is sung at that moment: `Person: text`; a leading `~` means nothing is sung there and this is the
    nearest text within 6 measures; empty = instrumental).
  - `strength`: statistical strength, not musical importance. strong: pattern information >= 35 bits and >= 4 scenes;
    medium: >= 28 bits and >= 3 occurrences; weak: the rest (all are far above chance, see `null_model.tsv`).
  - `shape`: `melodic`, `scalar` (mostly stepwise runs) or `cell` (few distinct pitches: tremolos, ostinati, trills). Descriptive only,
    used to arrange INDEX.md; nothing is excluded by it.
  - `meets_criteria`: >= 3 occurrences, >= 2 scenes, >= 5 notes.
- `occurrences.csv` — every occurrence of every leitmotif in one table.
- `excluded_stock.tsv` — candidates the reviewers excluded as *stock figures* (bare scale run, bare broken chord, trill,
  bare cadence formula), with their reason; nothing is excluded for its rhythm, and reviewers did not see durations.
- `null_model.tsv`, `null_model_semi_nr.tsv` — how many patterns the miner finds in surrogate music by length;
  `pool_summary.txt` — sizes of the candidate pool.
